Broadcast-style hockey, lacrosse, football, basketball and soccer with play-by-play from Bill Bender. Pick your CCL school (St. Charles, DeSales, Watterson or Hartley), play one game, or run a 6-game season with playoffs.

How to start

  1. 1Pick a sport belowPress its yellow Play button.
  2. 2Choose your school and levelRookie is easiest. Games start on Slow speed.
  3. 3PlayWASD to move, Space is your main button. Turn the sound on for the crowd and the horn.

Pick a sport

Hockey · 5 on 5

Breakaway '27

Body checks, slap shots, one-timers, power plays and a shootout if it's tied after overtime.

Play hockey

Controls

  • WASDSkate
  • SpaceShoot, hold for a slap shot. On defense, hold to body check, double-tap to switch player
  • 1–5Pass to that teammate, hold for a hard pass
  • E / QPass or poke check / deke or switch player
Lacrosse · 10 on 10

Ride & Clear

Faceoffs at the X, an 80-second shot clock, long poles and quick-stick finishes.

Play lacrosse

Controls

  • WASDRun
  • SpaceShoot, hold to shoot harder. On defense, tap to stick check, hold to body check
  • 1–9, 0Pass to that teammate (0 is the goalie), hold for a hard pass
  • E / QPass to the open man / dodge or switch player
Football · 11 on 11

Fourth & Long

Call plays, read the coverage, kick with the meter, and decide whether to go for two.

Play football

Controls

  • SpaceSnap, juke. On defense, big hit or dive
  • 1–5Throw to that receiver, hold Shift to lob it
  • WASDScramble and run
  • E / QStiff arm / spin move
Basketball · 5 on 5

Hardwood '27

Timing-based jump shots, dunks, blocks, steals, a 24-second shot clock and free throws.

Play basketball

Controls

  • WASDMove
  • SpaceShoot, let go at the top of the meter. On defense, tap to steal, hold to block
  • 1–5Pass to that teammate, hold for a bullet pass
  • E / QPass or steal / crossover or switch player
Soccer · 11 on 11

Final Third

Fouls with advantage, yellow and red cards, offside, corners, penalty kicks and playoff shootouts.

Play soccer

Controls

  • WASDDribble and run
  • SpaceShoot, hold for power. On defense, tap to tackle, hold to slide tackle
  • 1–9, 0Pass to that teammate, hold for a driven ball
  • E / QPass or press / skill move or switch player
