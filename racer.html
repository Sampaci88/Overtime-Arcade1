How to play

  • Race: beat Viper, Comet and Blitz to the finish line. Time trial: just you, the clock and your ghost. Hit every checkpoint either way.
  • Orange pads are boosts. Ramps jump gaps, so carry your speed.
  • Guard rails keep you on the track, but scraping them slows you down. Miss a jump and you go back to your last checkpoint. Press R any time to start the run over. Beat the gold time to earn a gold medal.
  • Your best run races with you as a see-through ghost.

Controls

  • W/↑ gas, S/↓ brake and reverse
  • A D steer, hold Space to drift
  • R reset (start the run over), Enter back to the last checkpoint
  • On a phone: steer on the left, gas and brake on the right.

Random tracks

Tap Random for a brand-new track. Every track has a code: send it to a friend and race the same track.
