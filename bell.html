Beat the Bell

How to play

  • Grab everything on your list from the hallway, then get to your classroom door before the bell.
  • Running is faster, but if a hall monitor sees you running, you get stopped and lose time.
  • From 4th period on, monitors also stop you if you walk right past them, unless you have a hall pass.
  • Grab Cardinal pins for bonus points and snacks to refill your energy.
  • Three tardies and it's detention. Make it through all 8 periods to finish the day.

Controls

  • WASD or arrow keys to walk
  • Hold Shift or Space to run
  • P to pause
  • On a phone: joystick on the left, RUN on the right. Turn it sideways for a bigger view.
