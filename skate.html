Shred Park

Tricks

  • Ollie: hold Space to crouch, let go to pop. Ride off a ramp or the stairs for big air.
  • Flips (J in the air): alone = Kickflip, → Heelflip, ← Pop Shove-it, ↑ 360 Flip, ↓ Varial Kickflip.
  • Grabs (hold K): Indy, ↑ Melon, ← Method, → Stalefish, ↓ Nose Grab.
  • Grinds (hold L as you come down on a rail or ledge): 50-50, ↑ Nosegrind, ↓ 5-0, ←/→ Boardslide.
  • Spins: hold ←/→ in the air: 180, 360, 540, 720 and the 900 on the big ramps.
  • Manuals: hold ↓ on the ground (↑ for a nose manual). Keep your balance with ←/→.

Combos

  • Land into a manual or a grind to keep a combo going. Your score is all the trick points times the number of tricks.
  • Land a flip before it's finished and you bail, and the combo is gone.
  • Gaps are bonus spots: the Table Gap, the Stair Set, the Funbox Gap, and Ledge to Rail (grind the funbox ledge, then jump onto the flat rail).
