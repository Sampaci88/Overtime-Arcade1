Extra Innings

Batting

  • Move the yellow bat circle over where the pitch will cross, then press Space to swing just as it gets there.
  • Early swings pull the ball, late swings go the other way. Get the bat circle a little under the ball to lift it.
  • Don't swing at balls: four of them is a walk.

Pitching

  • Pick a pitch: 1 fastball, 2 curveball, 3 changeup.
  • Aim with WASD, then press Space to start the meter and again in the green to throw where you aimed.
  • Mix speeds and work the corners to get strikeouts.
