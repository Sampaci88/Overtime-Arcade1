How to play

  • Aim with A D (hold Shift for fine aim), or just click or tap where you want to hit it, on the course or on the map. P aims at the pin. Pick a club with W S.
  • Swing: press Space to start, press again to set the power, then once more when the marker comes back to the white line. Early slices, late hooks.
  • Watch the wind, the lie and the slope of the green. The arrows on the green show which way it breaks.

Shots

  • Q/E backspin or topspin: backspin stops it on the green (a full wedge with backspin can spin back toward you), topspin rolls it out.
  • Rough costs distance, sand costs more (a sand wedge helps). Water and out of bounds cost a stroke.
  • G (or the Shot buttons) picks the shot type: Power (a power drive off the tee) goes farther but the sweet spot is smaller, Finesse is shorter and straighter, Approach lands soft, Backspin makes a wedge spin back, Stinger flies low under the wind, Punch stays under trees, Flop goes straight up and Bump & run rolls along the ground. F (or the Curve buttons) curves it: draw right to left, fade left to right, big draw and big fade bend it around doglegs. On a phone, tap Shot to open both. The dashed line shows where it will land.
  • V overhead view, R replay your last shot, Tab scorecard.

Modes

Stroke play for up to four golfers on one device, a tournament against a full field, a Daily 9 that's the same for everyone, a long drive contest, closest to the pin, 30 one-shot challenges with stars to earn, and Target Golf (glowing targets on a night range, great with friends). In the Pro Tour career, majors are four rounds from Thursday to Sunday with a cut, new pins every day, firmer weekend greens and sudden-death playoffs; sign sponsor deals, chase your rival and build a real handicap. Create your golfer in the locker, and press K (or Pause, then Photo mode) to frame and save a picture. Seven courses, from friendly parkland to a redwood forest, two ocean courses and two championship monsters: Azalea Pines and Iron Valley.
