How to play

  • Aim with A D (or the arrows). Pick a club with W S.
  • Swing: press Space to start, press again to set the power, then once more when the marker comes back to the white line. Early slices, late hooks.
  • Watch the wind, the lie and the slope of the green. The arrows on the green show which way it breaks.

Shots

  • Q/E backspin or topspin: backspin stops it on the green, topspin rolls it out.
  • Rough costs distance, sand costs more (a sand wedge helps). Water and out of bounds cost a stroke.
  • V overhead view, Tab scorecard.

Modes

Stroke play for up to four golfers on one device, a tournament against a full field, a Daily 9 that's the same for everyone, a long drive contest and closest to the pin. Three courses, all original.
